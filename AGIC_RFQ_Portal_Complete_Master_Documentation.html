1. System Overview & Architecture

The AGIC RFQ Portal is a full-stack platform designed for transparent, auditable procurement of heavy civil, structural steel, and infrastructure works. It allows the Chief Engineer to publish partitioned tenders with multiple job packages and enables contractors to quote for specific jobs with automated rate rollups and strict cutoff countdowns.

Core Capabilities:
  • Multi-Job Work Packages: Partitioning master tenders into distinct jobs (e.g. Fabrication, Erection, Sheeting, Precast RCC, Drainage).
  • Dynamic Bidder Selection: Contractors can select a subset or all jobs to quote.
  • Annexure II & III Rollup: Technical capability verification and itemized line-item pricing.
  • L1 Comparative Matrix: Automated ranking from lowest to highest bid with job-level filtering.
  • Immutable Audit History: Complete audit logging of all Chief Engineer evaluation decisions.

5. Step-by-Step User Workflows

Chief Engineer (CE) Workflow:
  1. Login: Enter mobile 9876543210 and password Admin@123 at /ce/login.
  2. Raise Tender: Click Raise New RFQ Tender & Jobs, enter technical parameters, EMD amount, and cutoff window.
  3. Add Jobs: Define multiple work packages (e.g. Steel Fabrication, Site Erection, Cladding) with estimated budgets.
  4. Publish: Click Publish Tender to make it visible on the public portal.
  5. Evaluate Submissions: When quotation window closes, open Tender Submissions to review the L1 ranking, filter by job, inspect applicant dossiers, and set status to ACCEPTED or REJECTED.
Contractor / Applicant Workflow:
  1. Register/Login: Login with mobile OTP or register firm with GSTIN and PAN at /applicant/login.
  2. Explore Tenders: Browse published tenders on the homepage and check quotation cutoff countdowns.
  3. 6-Step Submission Wizard:
    • Step 1: Enter authorized signatory name and covering letter date.
    • Step 2: Check/uncheck specific job packages to bid for.
    • Step 3: Enter Annexure II past capabilities and project values.
    • Step 4: Provide Annexure III unit rates mapped to selected jobs (auto-calculates grand total).
    • Step 5: Enter EMD transaction reference and attach PDF documents.
    • Step 6: Review computed quotation breakdown and confirm submission.
  4. Track Bids: Open My Submitted Quotations to view submission dossiers and CE review decisions.

7. Security, RBAC & Cutoff Guardrails

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Applicants can only view their own submissions. Competitor bids and pricing remain hidden.
  • Quotation Window Enforcement: Submissions attempted before opening or after cutoff timestamps are rejected with 400 Bad Request.
  • Multi-Job Validation: If a tender defines job packages, applicants must select at least one active job.
  • Immutable Audit Trail: Every status change made by the Chief Engineer is permanently logged in application_status_history.

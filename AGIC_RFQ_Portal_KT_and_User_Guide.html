2. Chief Engineer (CE) Training Guide

This section guides Chief Engineers and Procuring Officers through tender publishing, pre-bid handling, and evaluation.

1 Login to CE Administrative Portal

Navigate to http://localhost:5173/ce/login.

Use the credentials: Mobile: 9876543210 | Password: Admin@123.

2 Raise a New RFQ Tender

From the CE Dashboard, click "Raise New RFQ Tender & Jobs" (/ce/tenders/new).

  • Tender Identification: Enter Tender Reference Number (e.g. RFQ/AGIC/2026/INFRA-02) and work title.
  • Scope of Work & Specs: Detail standards (IS 800), surface prep, total pieces, element types, and weight estimates.
  • EMD & Quotation Window: Specify the Earnest Money Deposit amount and the START and END cutoff timestamps.
3 Configure Work Packages / Jobs

In Section 2 of the form, add multiple job packages under the tender:

  • Job Code: e.g. JOB-01, JOB-02.
  • Job Name: e.g. Structural Steel Shop Fabrication, Tower Erection & Bolting.
  • Category & Quantities: e.g. Steel / 250 MT.
  • Estimated Cost (₹): Budget allocation. The form automatically computes the cumulative budget across all jobs in real time.
4 Attach Documents & Publish

Attach the official RFQ PDF document and click "Publish Tender with Job Packages". The tender is now live on the public portal.

5 Answer Pre-Bid Queries

Open the tender details page to view queries submitted by bidders. Type the official departmental response and click "Publish Reply".

6 Evaluate Bids in the L1 Comparative Matrix

When the quotation window closes, open "View All Submissions" (/ce/tenders/{id}/submissions):

  • Bids are automatically ranked from lowest (👑 L1) to highest.
  • Use the "Filter by Job" dropdown to inspect quotes for individual work packages.
  • Click "Evaluate Dossier" on any applicant to review their Annexure II past capabilities, Annexure III rates, and EMD receipt.
  • Set the evaluation decision (ACCEPTED or REJECTED) with justification remarks. The system logs an audit trail in the database.

3. Contractor / Applicant Training Guide

This section guides contractor firms on onboarding, tender discovery, job selection, and submitting compliant quotations.

1 Contractor Registration & OTP Login

Go to http://localhost:5173/applicant/login.

  • New Firm: Click Register New Contracting Firm, enter company name, GSTIN, PAN, MD/CEO name, and mobile number.
  • Existing Firm: Enter registered mobile number (e.g. 9111222333) and verify using the instant 6-digit OTP.
2 Discover Active Tenders

On the homepage, review published tenders. Check the live Ticking Countdown Timer to verify the time remaining before the quotation cutoff.

3 6-Step Quotation Submission Wizard

Click "Submit Quotation Online" (/applicant/apply/{tenderId}):

  1. Step 1: Signatory Details — Enter authorized signatory name and designation (e.g. Managing Director).
  2. Step 2: Job Package Selection — Check/uncheck specific jobs. You can quote for one, multiple, or all work packages.
  3. Step 3: Annexure II (Capabilities) — Enter past similar works executed, client names, and project cost in ₹ Lakhs.
  4. Step 4: Annexure III (Line Items & Rates) — Enter your rate per unit for each line item mapped to your selected jobs. The grand total is calculated automatically.
  5. Step 5: EMD Payment & Docs — Enter bank UTR / DD payment reference and upload PDF verification proofs.
  6. Step 6: Review & Final Submit — Review the computed quote breakdown across your selected jobs and click "Confirm & Submit Quotation".
4 Track Status in "My Submitted Quotations"

Open /applicant/my-applications to view your submitted bids, selected job badges, and the Chief Engineer's evaluation decision.

4. Multi-Job Work Packages Explained

How Multi-Job Bidding Works:

A single master tender (e.g. Administrative Towers Construction) can be partitioned by the Chief Engineer into multiple specialized work packages:

  • JOB-01: Structural Steel Fabrication (Shop work, Shot blasting, Primer) — Est. Budget: ₹2.15 Cr
  • JOB-02: Site Crane Erection & High-Tensile Torque Bolting — Est. Budget: ₹85 Lakhs
  • JOB-03: Standing Seam Metal Roofing & Wall Cladding — Est. Budget: ₹1.40 Cr

Contractor Flexibility: A specialized steel fabrication firm can bid for only JOB-01, while a turnkey contractor can bid for all 3 jobs in a single unified submission docket.

5. FAQ & KT Troubleshooting

Q1: What happens if an applicant tries to submit after the countdown expires?

The backend API strictly rejects submissions attempted after quotation_to_date with a 400 Bad Request: Quotation window is closed. The online submit button is automatically disabled.

Q2: Can contractors see other contractors' quoted prices?

No. The portal enforces role isolation. Applicants can only view their own quotations. Competitor pricing is strictly hidden to prevent price collusion.

Q3: How does the Chief Engineer determine L1?

The Comparative Evaluation Matrix automatically ranks all submitted bids from lowest quoted amount (👑 L1) to highest. The CE can filter by specific job package to see L1 rankings for individual work components.

Q4: How do I restart the servers if Antigravity is closed?

Double-click the START_PORTAL.bat shortcut on your Desktop. It launches both servers independently and opens the portal in your web browser.

1. System Overview & Tech Stack

The AGIC RFQ Portal coordinates high-value infrastructure and engineering tenders. It provides strict quotation window cutoffs, multi-job partitioning, itemized rate submissions, automated L1 comparative rankings, and complete audit trail logging.

4. Operational Workflows

Chief Engineer (CE) Lifecycle:
  1. Login with mobile 9876543210 / Admin@123.
  2. Click Raise New RFQ Tender & Jobs to configure tender specifications, multiple job packages with budgets, and cutoff dates.
  3. Publish the tender. Contractors can now view the tender on the home page and submit pre-bid queries.
  4. Upon window closing, review the L1 Comparative Evaluation Matrix sorted by quoted amounts, filter by job packages, inspect Annexure II and III, and record evaluation decisions with audit remarks.
Contractor / Applicant Lifecycle:
  1. Register contracting firm with mobile number, GSTIN, PAN, and corporate details.
  2. Browse published tenders on the portal with live countdown timers.
  3. Complete the 6-step online quotation submission wizard:
    • Step 1: Signatory & covering letter details.
    • Step 2: Work Package Selection (Select one, multiple, or all jobs).
    • Step 3: Annexure II past technical & financial capability.
    • Step 4: Annexure III itemized unit rates tagged to selected jobs.
    • Step 5: EMD deposit details & document upload.
    • Step 6: Review computed quotation totals and lock submission into the database.
  4. Track submitted bids and view CE evaluation decisions in My Submitted Quotations.

6. Security & Guardrails

  • Role-Based Access Control: Applicants can only view their own submissions. Competitor quotations remain hidden.
  • Quotation Window Enforcement: Submissions attempted before opening or after cutoff timestamps are rejected with 400 Bad Request.
  • Multi-Job Validation: If a tender defines job packages, applicants must select at least one active job.
  • Immutable Audit Trail: Every status change made by the Chief Engineer is permanently logged in application_status_history.
